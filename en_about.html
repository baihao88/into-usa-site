Company Profile
Established in 2017, Into USA has chosen Washington DC, the capital of the United States, as its headquarters, providing convenient access to global clients and US government agencies<

Into USA is committed to providing efficient and professional services for global clients when traveling to the United States. Through the efforts of its professional team, we help clients solve various problems during their trip to the United States and achieve a smooth travel experience.

Into America focuses on the field of services for studying in the United States. Whether it's studying abroad, visiting schools, working, or traveling, we can provide comprehensive support and services to ensure smooth life and work for our clients in the United States.
Ben Hermesh

Senior expert in immigration field

As the founder of the Into US program, Ben Hermesh has become a leading figure in the global talent immigration and visa field due to his deep understanding and extensive experience in US immigration policies.


Quick success cases

Through his own personal experience, Ben Hermesh successfully obtained the Outstanding Talent (EB1a) immigration approval in just 12 days, demonstrating his outstanding abilities in immigration planning and execution.


Comprehensive service expertise

Ben Hermesh is not only skilled in outstanding talent immigration, but also proficient in investment immigration, comprehensive visa services, and US investment and real estate, providing personalized one-stop immigration solutions for clients.

Teacher Sherry

Educational background and professional experience

Sherry holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) degree from a prestigious American university and has served as an internal admissions management teacher at multiple universities, accumulating rich experience in the education industry and providing professional guidance to students.


Core Responsibilities and Services

As a core team member of Into USA, Sherry is responsible for enrollment planning, application material optimization, interview coaching, and cross-cultural communication support, helping students improve their admission opportunities and smoothly integrate into American study life.


Cultural adaptation and academic development support

With over 10 years of experience living in the United States and a deep understanding of the American education system, Sherry provides support to students and their parents in terms of life adaptation and cultural differences, helping students with their future academic and career development.
